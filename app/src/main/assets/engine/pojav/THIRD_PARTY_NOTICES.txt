This Sally Launcher patch includes Pojav-derived Android JNI bridge source under
app/src/main/cpp/pojav/, the patched GLFW classes archive
lwjgl-glfw-classes.jar, and Android-native LWJGL/GL4ES/OpenAL libraries under
app/src/main/jniLibs/. Native bridge source is compiled into libpojavexec.so by
Sally Launcher's CMake build. The patched Java classes and native libraries are
matched to the supplied upstream snapshot; they are not guaranteed to work with
all Minecraft/LWJGL versions or every Android device.
